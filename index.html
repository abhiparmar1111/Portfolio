About

Engineering AI that survives contact with users

I'm an AI/ML Engineer who builds LLM and Generative-AI features end to end — from retrieval and prompting to evaluation, deployment, and serving cost.

My work sits at the application layer of AI: retrieval-augmented generation over vector search, agents that use tools through the Model Context Protocol, and document pipelines that scrape, OCR, embed, and index at scale. It's grounded in machine-learning fundamentals — NLP, embeddings, and model evaluation — which is what lets me pick the right approach, tune retrieval, and measure whether a system is actually getting better.

Before AI, I spent a year as a backend developer building REST APIs and business software. That foundation is why the AI I ship is properly engineered, not just prototyped.
